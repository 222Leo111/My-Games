🌿 Little Worlds Online

A tiny 3D hangout where you and your friends can walk around, chat, and roleplay together.

Choose “Create a world” to host, or join a friend's world code.

Online play uses PeerJS's public signaling service for connection setup. This demo has no accounts or private server; anyone with the host code may be able to join. Only share it with people you trust.
